# Changelog

Every push to `main` builds the game, deploys it to GitHub Pages and, when the version in `package.json` is new, tags `vX.Y.Z` and publishes a GitHub release with the notes below.

## [0.1.0]

- First playable region: Osaka.
- Play as Irving or Moeno (switch any time from the START menu). Mui follows you around.
- Three Osaka stops: the U.S. Consulate General Osaka-Kobe (notary for the California partnership declaration), the fingerprint office (FBI prints, the pricey option) and the call with the company and lawyer at the cafe or at home (social security certificate of coverage request).
- Route choices for bringing Moeno (California domestic partnership, pareja de hecho in Spain, or her own visa) and for the FBI fingerprints. The checklist changes with each choice.
- Journal with a journey progress bar, every stage from Osaka to Valencia, and tap-to-tick for real-life progress.
- Moeno's Osaka missions are marked as coming next.
