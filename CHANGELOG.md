# Changelog

Every push to `main` builds the ROM. When `VERSION` holds a version that hasn't been released yet, the workflow tags `vX.Y.Z` and publishes a GitHub release with the `.gba` attached and the notes below.

## [0.1.0]

- First playable Game Boy Advance ROM. Runs on mGBA, other emulators and flash carts; progress is kept in battery save (SRAM).
- Osaka overworld with the U.S. Consulate General Osaka-Kobe, Osaka Station, home, Cafe Nomado, Fingerprint Service Osaka, the canal, the castle, the tower and a takoyaki stand.
- Play as Irving or Moeno (switch from the START menu). Mui follows you around.
- The call with the company and the lawyer (cafe or home): the company submits the social security certificate of coverage request, and you pick how Moeno comes (California domestic partnership, pareja de hecho in Spain, or her own visa) and where to get FBI fingerprints.
- Consulate notary for the California partnership declaration, and the pricey FBI fingerprints in Osaka.
- Journal (SELECT): journey progress bar, route choices and every stage from Osaka to Valencia, with ticks for real-life progress.
- Moeno's Osaka missions are marked as coming next.
